## Decisions and tradeoffs

The source supports the documented observation or reported choice. Proposed alternatives, inferred costs, and transfer questions are editorial synthesis unless explicitly attributed.

### 1. Delete unnecessary decoration before tuning the remaining system.

Documented personal editing process; tradeoff analysis

**Alternative:** Keep every generated flourish and polish each independently.

**Tradeoff:** Subtraction can improve coherence and repeated use, while excessive removal can erase helpful feedback or identity.

Evidence: [Source 1](https://www.raphaelsalaja.com/library/what-the-model-misses)

Decision ID: `raphael-salaja-decision-1`

### 2. Reconstruct one small interaction and vary one parameter at a time.

Documented learning method

**Alternative:** Copy an entire product or collect visual references without implementing them.

**Tradeoff:** A narrow exercise reveals causal relationships but does not teach the original product’s broader constraints.

Evidence: [Source 1](https://www.raphaelsalaja.com/library/on-copying)

Decision ID: `raphael-salaja-decision-2`

### 3. Shorten motion as frequency rises, and consider faster exits than entrances.

Documented heuristics; validation remains necessary

**Alternative:** Apply one default duration to all transitions.

**Tradeoff:** Less waiting serves expert repetition; excessive speed can hide state relationships or impair perception. Test the actual task instead of treating his values as universal constants.

Evidence: [Source 1](https://www.raphaelsalaja.com/library/a-note-on-timing)

Decision ID: `raphael-salaja-decision-3`

### 4. Combine a shared motion preset with component-level overrides and reduced-motion handling.

Documented API choices; tradeoff analysis

**Alternative:** Make every morphing interaction bespoke.

**Tradeoff:** Defaults promote consistency while overrides preserve context; too many exceptions can fragment the system. Composition on accessible primitives is a starting point, not a completed audit.

Evidence: [Source 1](https://cambio.raphaelsalaja.com/)

Decision ID: `raphael-salaja-decision-4`

## Practice

Original exercises inspired by the sources, not practitioner-prescribed methods.

### 1. Practice the judgment

Choose one generated component you own. Record five repetitions, remove nonessential decoration, align its spacing and motion, and rewrite vague state copy. Compare three durations while keeping the curve fixed.

**Deliverable:** A before/after recording, a token table, and a decision log explaining every deletion and retained animation.

**Evaluate:** Can another person identify the current state without explanation? Does the fifth repetition still feel responsive? Does interruption work, and is reduced motion usable? Can every flourish justify its cost or intentional expressive purpose?
