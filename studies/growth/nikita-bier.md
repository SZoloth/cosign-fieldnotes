## Decisions and tradeoffs

The source supports the documented observation or reported choice. Proposed alternatives, inferred costs, and transfer questions are editorial synthesis unless explicitly attributed.

### 1. Constrain expression to support the intended experience

Teens sought social feedback through awkward and sometimes harmful anonymous messaging. For TBH, use authored positive polls rather than unrestricted anonymous text. Bier reports strong adoption and message volume; not independent proof of wellbeing outcomes.

**Tradeoff:** Reduce expressive freedom to constrain harmful interactions.

**Limit:** The lesson concerns a specific social product; safety claims need separate validation.

**Transfer question / exercise:** Could a carefully constrained interaction deliver the desired value with less misuse?

Evidence: [Source 1](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/how-to-consistently-go-viral-nikita-bier)

Decision ID: `nb-d1`

### 2. Make value immediate and demonstrable

Dupe contained several shopping features and a useful price-comparison function. Focus on that function and make it accessible through a memorable URL interaction. Bier reports viral videos and revenue growth, but supplies no audited figures.

**Tradeoff:** Remove breadth and invest in a distinctive entry point.

**Limit:** Do not treat his approximate three-second heuristic as a universal SLA.

**Transfer question / exercise:** What setup could move after the first safe, meaningful result?

Evidence: [Source 1](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/how-to-consistently-go-viral-nikita-bier)

Decision ID: `nb-d2`

### 3. Test a viable network condition

A social product can fail because the necessary people never arrive together. Use a narrow community test with the conditions required to experience value. The thread offers a testing heuristic, not a controlled outcome.

**Tradeoff:** A concentrated manual test is not a scalable acquisition channel.

**Limit:** Its suggested number of community tests cannot establish a universal stopping rule.

**Transfer question / exercise:** What must be true for your test to distinguish lack of value from an absent network?

Evidence: [Source 1](https://threadreaderapp.com/scrolly/1481118406749220868)

Decision ID: `nb-d3`

### 4. Optimize the experiment process

Consumer ideas have uncertain appeal and teams can lose time in ambiguous launches. Improve repeatable access to a relevant audience and define what counts as an informative result. A practitioner recommendation rather than demonstrated comparative probability of success.

**Tradeoff:** Testing infrastructure competes with building another product concept.

**Limit:** The thread itself notes luck, timing and outliers.

**Transfer question / exercise:** Which confounder would let you explain away a disappointing result afterward?

Evidence: [Source 1](https://threadreaderapp.com/scrolly/1481118406749220868)

Decision ID: `nb-d4`

## Practice

Original exercises inspired by the sources, not practitioner-prescribed methods.

### 1. Find latent demand

Observe a harmless existing workaround for a consumer task. Describe the desired value without proposing a product first.

**Deliverable:** Three observed behaviors, one problem statement and two alternative explanations.

**Evaluate:** Observation is distinguished from inference. No demographic stereotype is treated as a fact.

### 2. Design a credible small-community test

Specify the minimum relevant participants, value condition, consent process and outcome for a hypothetical social feature.

**Deliverable:** A test protocol with confounders and guardrails.

**Evaluate:** Tests real value, not compelled invitations. Protects contact information. Does not use popularity as a wellbeing metric.

### 3. Cut the path to first value

Sketch the current and proposed path for a simple utility. Identify which steps are necessary for safety and which can move later.

**Deliverable:** Two flows and a rationale for every removed step.

**Evaluate:** Retains necessary consent and authentication. Defines value behaviorally.
