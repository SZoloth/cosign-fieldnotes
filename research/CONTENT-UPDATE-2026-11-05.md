## Read it without the app

The [study library](../studies/README.md) contains one readable Markdown page per practitioner, with original source links, actual access, attribution, decisions, tradeoffs, communication observations, exercises, and limits. [Comparisons](../studies/comparisons.md) and the [discovery queue](../studies/discovery.md) are separate.

The portable JSON retains companion URLs, source locators, speakers, publishers, confidence notes, and caveats. Validation now rejects duplicate people and decision IDs, missing attribution, future publication dates, unregistered evidence references, and incomplete exercises instead of silently dropping records.

## What this does not establish

These are selected studies from larger nomination lists, not an exhaustive review or professional ranking. Corporate announcements are not independent audits. Edited interviews are not sole-authored prose. Team products are not attributed to one person without evidence. Dated implementation choices are not treated as current specifications. Suggested tradeoffs, counterexamples, and exercises are editorial synthesis rather than the practitioners' own instructions.
