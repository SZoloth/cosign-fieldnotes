# Public research data

These files contain selected public-source readings and original editorial synthesis. They intentionally preserve source access, attribution, and limits. They contain no private correspondence or user notes.

The application normalizes these records into `dist/data/collection.json`. The source schemas vary across research cohorts; `scripts/build-collection.mjs` is the adapter. Preserve originals and their provenance rather than flattening away uncertainty.

Research date: 2026-11-05. Historical role references describe the dated source, not necessarily a person's current position. Coverage is bounded and is not a claim that a person's full output was reviewed.
